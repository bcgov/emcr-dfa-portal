Project Eligibility Appeal Process
If you believe that the DFA program incorrectly determined the eligibility for this project, whether it is for the entire project or a specific cost(s), you may appeal the determination within 60 days of the date on the notice being appealed. Submitting an appeal does not guarantee that the DFA determination will be overturned. Appeal determinations may overturn or uphold the DFA determination being appealed.
