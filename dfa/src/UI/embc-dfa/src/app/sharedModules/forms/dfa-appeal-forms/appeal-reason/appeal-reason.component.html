Your application for DFA was determined to be ineligible for one or more reasons outlined in your DFA ineligibility letter. To appeal an eligibility determination, you must provide reasoning as to why you believe your application should have been determined to be eligible.

Please ensure you have reviewed your ineligibility letter prior to submitting an appeal.

To appeal DFA amount determinations, you must provide reasoning as to why you believe your application should have resulted in a greater payment amount. Appeals staff recommend reviewing your Field Manager report and DFA payment letter, listing the costs or tasks you believe are missing or miscalculated in those documents, and stating why you believe they are missing or miscalculated. Please note, DFA uses industry standard rates at the time of the event to determine repair costs and the cost of a basic model to determine replacement costs. Invoices can be helpful supporting documentation, but DFA amounts are not necessarily determined by invoice amounts.
